[h1]KenshiMP — co-op & PvP multiplayer for Kenshi[/h1]

[b]BETA — back up your saves before playing. Report bugs on GitHub (link below).[/b]

Play Kenshi with your friends. Every player keeps their own squad, faction, base and towns; you see each other's characters move, fight, build and trade in real time. Player factions can be at peace, allied or at war.

[b]Subscribe, tick KenshiMP in the launcher, play: nothing to install or run, no game file changed. No RE_Kenshi needed on Steam.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Smooth movement of the other players' characters (walk, run, sprint, combat), with their names above their heads
[*] Real combat between players: damage is computed by the attacker and applied by the victim's owner, never twice
[*] The host's world is shared: NPCs, animals, time of day, weather, pause (speed locked at x1)
[*] Buildings and towns of each player, construction, destruction, doors
[*] Inventories, chests, looting, items dropped on the ground, worn backpacks, traders' money; town chests hold the same things for everybody
[*] Direct trading between players in the game's own trade window
[*] Diplomacy: war / peace / alliance per player, from the Multiplayer window or the chat (/war, /peace, /ally)
[*] Chat (Enter), other players on the world map, injuries, lost limbs and prosthetics, hunger
[*] Join through a Steam invitation, straight from the title screen: no IP address, no port, no VPN
[*] Multiplayer window (title screen, pause menu or F4) in the game's style, English and French
[*] Join a game already running: GO TO (or /goto) brings your squad next to your friends wherever your save left it
[*] Optional load sharing (load_sharing=1, experimental): a player far from the host simulates their own surroundings, so the host's PC only runs its own region
[/list]

[h2]Requirements (every player)[/h2]
[list]
[*] Kenshi on Steam, as Steam installs it: nothing else needed
[*] GOG, or if you already use it: [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kenshi/mods/847]RE_Kenshi[/url] 0.3.5 or later (then also NVIDIA PhysX System Software 9.10.0513 and Kenshi's PhysX DLLs next to RE_Kenshi's Kenshi_x64.exe, details in GUIDE.md). Players with and without RE_Kenshi can play together.
[*] Exactly the same mod list, in the same order, and the same KenshiMP version (the host refuses otherwise and tells you what differs)
[/list]

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Subscribe, then tick KenshiMP in the launcher's Mods tab. That is all (untick it to play single-player as before). Borderless window mode is recommended.
[*] Host: click MULTIPLAYER under the title menu (or press F4), then HOST, then STEAM FRIENDS > INVITE next to your friends. Then NEW GAME or CONTINUE.
[*] Friends: accept the Steam invitation (or "Join game" on the host's Steam profile), then NEW GAME (a fresh game is recommended) or CONTINUE. Click GO TO next to the host in the Multiplayer window (or type /goto) to meet them.
[*] Without Steam (GOG copies, LAN, VPN): ADVANCED SETTINGS in the Multiplayer window, enter the host's address, port and password, JOIN BY ADDRESS. The host opens TCP port 47000 or you use a gaming VPN (Radmin, ZeroTier, Tailscale).
[/olist]
Settings are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\kenshi\KenshiMP\kenshimp.cfg.

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*] Each client keeps its own save (saved as <name>_MP, your solo save is untouched); the host's world (towns destroyed or taken over, unique NPCs, broken town doors, town chests) is applied to it while playing.
[*] All players need the same KenshiMP version: it is shown in the Multiplayer window title (v18). Steam updates everybody automatically.
[*] Up to 8 players; the host must stay connected (no host migration).
[*] Other code mods that change the same game functions may conflict.
[/list]
