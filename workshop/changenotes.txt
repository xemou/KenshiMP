v19 - fixes from your feedback (protocol 19: everybody must update, the host refuses older versions)

Thank you for the reviews. This update works on what you reported:

- Coming back to the host's world after being far away: your own NPCs now stay until the host's have
  arrived (up to 45 s), instead of leaving an empty town for a while. The host loads the zones around a
  returning player straight away.
- Going back to your own world: the NPCs removed meanwhile come back (same character, position, looks and
  gear), on top of what the game creates by itself. Their inventory and money are not restored.
- Looks: other characters' looks are now copied exactly. Before, anything the original did not define kept a
  random value, so people looked different (and differently after each reload).
- NPC items and money for non-host players: if the host's description of an NPC never reached you, your
  game now asks again (NPCs within 60 units, up to 4 times).
- If the host's zone around a player never loads, that player simulates their own surroundings after 25 s
  instead of standing in an empty town.
- Test builds log a census of the surroundings (NPCs, host NPCs, zone, furnished buildings).
- New COMPATIBILITY.md: what is known about other mods (same mod list, same order, for everybody).

Still to check in real games (tell me!): NPC items and money for non-host players, looks with race mods
(VRoid Race Face+ and others: if a mod does not work, send me its name and the /report folder).
Known limits: no host migration, speed locked at x1, carrying or caging another player's character is blocked.

v18 - first public beta (protocol 18)

- Nothing to install: subscribe, tick KenshiMP in the launcher, play (no RE_Kenshi needed on Steam)
- Join through a Steam invitation from the title screen, then NEW GAME or CONTINUE; IP / port / password
  only under ADVANCED SETTINGS (GOG, LAN, VPN)
- Co-op & PvP multiplayer for Kenshi, up to 8 players, host's world shared (NPCs, time, weather, towns)
- Join a game already running: GO TO in the Multiplayer window (or /goto) brings your squad next to a friend
- Town chests hold the same things for everybody; looting is validated by the host
- Other players' names always shown above their characters, in their chat colour
- Player numbers kept between host restarts (same name = same number)
- Version shown in the Multiplayer window title: everybody must have the same one; a player with another
  version is told plainly who has to update (and the host is told who could not join)

- The host's save is backed up automatically each time a hosted game starts (KenshiMP\backups, last 5 kept)
- /report (or BUG REPORT in the Multiplayer window): logs, settings and session summary in one folder on the desktop

Back up your saves. Bug reports: github.com/xemou/KenshiMP (attach the /report folder, zipped).
